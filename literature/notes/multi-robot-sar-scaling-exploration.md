## Abstract (verbatim)

Autonomous multi-robot systems provide a safer alternative to human responders in hazardous disaster environments. This work evaluates a multi-objective grid-based exploration strategy that maximizes coverage while minimizing redundancy, exploration time, and constraint violations. Four metaheuristic algorithms—Simulated Annealing (SA), Genetic Algorithm (GA), Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), and Aquila Optimization Algorithm (AOA)—are assessed across three environments of increasing complexity. While AOA performs best in small-scale settings, its effectiveness declines as the search space grows. SA and PSO achieve competitive coverage in medium scenarios but struggle with refinement and penalty avoidance at larger scales. Overall, GA exhibits the strongest scalability, maintaining a superior balance between coverage and constraints in complex environments, indicating that populationbased evolutionary strategies are more robust for large-scale disaster response.
