## Abstract (verbatim)

Multi-cooperative Vehicle Fleets have been the subject of many research papers and rigorous tests over the years. In this paper we aim to implement a novel multi-level optimization architecture, that optimizes both path and task allocation through the use of state-of-the-art optimization techniques and algorithms on Disaster relief operations. This architecture takes into account Weather Conditions, like Wildfires, Blizzards, Floods, and many more. Due to the severity and sensitivity of the context, it is very crucial that the applied Optimization techniques take into account these weather conditions and their effects on a vehicle fleets’ Speed, fuel consumption, distance traveled, task allocation, chosen path and the overall success of the operation. The main algorithms covered in this paper are Simulated Annealing (SA), Genetic Algorithm (GA), Discrete Grey Wolf and Artificial Bee Colony (ABC).
