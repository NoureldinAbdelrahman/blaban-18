## Abstract (verbatim)

Autonomous Intersection Management (AIM) enables signal-free coordination of Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) to improve traffic efficiency while maintaining safety. This paper presents a priority-aware AIM framework formulated as a mixed discrete–continuous optimization problem using a conflict-point abstraction. Multiple metaheuristic algorithms are compared for vehicle ordering and speed optimization. A Hybrid ACO–PSO method is proposed to separately optimize the discrete passing sequence and continuous vehicle speeds. Experimental results demonstrate improved convergence stability, robustness, and computational efficiency, making the hybrid approach a balanced solution for priorityaware intersection scheduling.
