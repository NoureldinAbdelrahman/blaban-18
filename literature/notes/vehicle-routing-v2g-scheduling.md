## Abstract (verbatim)

The paper investigates the Vehicle Routing and Charging/Discharging Scheduling Problem for an electric vehicle fleet with Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) technology for a given network. Moreover, it compares different algorithms to maximize the revenue generated by V2G through the charging and discharging of electric vehicles, while ensuring they reach their destination by a given deadline. In order to reduce the high complexity of the problem, four different metaheuristic algorithms are introduced: Simulated Annealing (SA), Genetic Algorithm (GA), Discrete Grey Wolf Optimizer (DGWO), and Shuffled Frog Leaping Algorithm (SFLA). The performance of the implemented metaheuristic algorithms is also examined through multiple experiments with different tuning parameter. The performance of each algorithm has been found to vary significantly. The research also provides insights in effective configuration for the Vehicle Routing and Charging/Discharging Scheduling Problem for Vehicle-to-Grid with a focus on profit maximization.
