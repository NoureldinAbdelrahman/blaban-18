# Wednesday Meeting Pack: Settle the Idea and Start the Proposal

## Status

`proposals/use_case.md`, `idea_1.md`, `idea_2.md` are **discussion drafts**, not
team decisions. The meeting approves, amends, or replaces them.

## Fixed requirements

- Deadline: **Thursday, 1 October 2026, 23:59**; max **two pages including references**.
- Two **different** ideas, first = priority; the course team allocates one.
- Five recent articles (**2019–2026**), at least one supporting each idea.
- Template: [`docs/templates/proposal_template.md`](templates/proposal_template.md).

## Bring to the meeting

1. Three completed archive readings ([assignments](../literature/reviews/reading_assignments.md)).
2. Matching rows in the [literature log](../literature/reviews/literature_review_log.md).
3. One external Scholar candidate or one written rejection reason.
4. A first choice and a backup for the project idea.
5. Any blocking feasibility question.

## Agenda (60 minutes)

1. **Rules and deadline — 5 min.** Confirm deadline, page limit, two ideas, references.
2. **Paper lightning round — 20 min.** Each member presents their best paper in two
   minutes: problem, agents, method, result, supported idea.
3. **Option comparison — 20 min.** Score A–C below, 1–5 per cell.
4. **Decision — 10 min.** Lock priority idea, backup idea, and the five papers.
5. **Ownership — 5 min.** Assign literature review, Idea 1, Idea 2, references, PDF export.

## Draft options

### A — Agricultural swarm optimization with learning-assisted strategy selection

- Ground-robot fleet monitors a field (soil, weeds, pests); joint assignment and routing.
- Minimize energy and makespan; respect sensor match, battery, rows, windows, precedence.
- Novel step: deep reinforcement learning steers a population-based swarm optimizer.

### B — Planetary-surface exploration under energy, terrain, and communication limits

- Heterogeneous rovers plus a lander relay explore a Mars-like site.
- Maximize science return; minimize energy and makespan; survive a rover failure.
- Novel step: multi-objective coevolutionary planning with recharging and replanning.

### C — Neutral archive-style alternative

- Heterogeneous delivery, warehouse, or airport service fleet.
- Joint allocation and routing under capacity and scheduling limits.
