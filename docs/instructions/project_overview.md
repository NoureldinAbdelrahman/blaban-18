# MCTR 1021 — Project Overview

> Parsed from `project_overview.pdf`.

**Institution:** German University in Cairo (GUC)
**Faculty:** Faculty of Engineering & Materials Science (EMS)
**Department:** Mechatronics Department (MCTR)
**Course:** MCTR 1021 — Optimization Techniques for Multi-Cooperative Systems
**Term:** Winter 2026

## Aims of the Project

- Generate optimal or near-optimal solutions for real-life problems related to
  multi-agent cooperative systems via meta-heuristic optimization techniques.
- Students can choose projects under the umbrella of **multi-agent cooperative
  systems in robotics and/or autonomous systems** (e.g. autonomous vehicles,
  UAVs, ... etc).

## Hardware

- No hardware is required in this project context.

## Programming Languages Allowed

- Choose either **MATLAB** or **Python**.
- All techniques, visualizations and simulations will be implemented using the
  chosen language.
- The chosen language **will not be changed** after the implementation of the
  first algorithm.
- _Project decision: this repository is scaffolded for **Python**._

## Teams

- Each team is composed of **4–6 members** (no exceptions).
- Students not assigned to teams by the deadline of team registration will be
  randomly clustered/assigned.
- No change to project teams (interchange or exchange of project) will be
  accepted under any circumstances.

## Project Selection

- Each team reviews **5 recent scientific articles** (publication date between
  **2019–2026**) related to the themes above.
- A file with previous publications from the course is provided (see
  `literature/papers/`).
- From the reviewed papers, each team selects **2 different ideas** and submits
  project proposals (1st idea = first priority).
- The proposal is submitted in the template provided on CMS (see
  `docs/templates/proposal_template.md`).
- The proposal must be **maximum 2 pages**.
- The course team reviews both ideas and assigns one to the team.

## Submission Deadline

- **Proposals:** Thursday, 1 October 2026, at 23:59.

## Project Milestones

The overall maximum grade for the project is **40%** of the course grade. The
project is split into **6 milestones**.

| Milestone | Brief Description | Weight | Deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1: Proposal | Two-page proposal of 2 project ideas within the theme scope. | 3% | Week 4 — Thu, 1 Oct 2026 |
| 2: Formulation | Two-page literature survey of ≥ 5 relevant articles in the 6 preceding years (2018–2026) and problem formulation as code. | 5% | Week 5 — Wed, 8 Oct 2026 |
| 3: SA | Implementation of Simulated Annealing (SA) on your problem. | 6% | Week 7 — Mon, 19 Oct 2026 |
| 4: GA | Implementation of Genetic Algorithm (GA) on your problem. | 8% | Week 10 — Wed, 11 Nov 2026 |
| 5: PSO / ACO / WOA / GWO | Implementation of either Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA) or Grey Wolf Optimization (GWO). | 8% | Week 13 — Wed, 2 Dec 2026 |
| 6: New Algorithm + Final Report | Implementation of a new meta-heuristic population-based optimization algorithm (not taught in the course); final project report (6-page IEEE paper format, LaTeX); poster; optional ML bonus. | 10% | Week 15 — Thu, 17 Dec 2026 |

### Grading Criteria

- Quality of all submitted material.
- For code: scalability, modularity, documentation (well commented).
- For results: analysis of all results (numerical, graphical, performance
  metrics, parametric studies, statistical studies) in light of the problem;
  commenting on trends; recommendations from the analysis and discussion.
- For reporting: clarity of presentation and professional discussion with
  multiple aids (block diagrams, flowcharts, figures, tables, graphs, charts).
